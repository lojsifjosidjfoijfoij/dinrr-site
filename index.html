Frequently Asked Questions

How do I share with my family?
Go to Settings → Create family. Share the code with your family members and they can join under Settings → Join family.

How do I get AI ingredient suggestions?
Add a meal name to any day, then tap the sparkle icon. This feature requires dinrr+.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Go to iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → dinrr+.

I lost my data — what do I do?
Make sure you're signed in with the same account. Your data is tied to your account.
