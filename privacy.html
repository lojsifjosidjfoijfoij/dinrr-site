Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2, 2026

dinrr is operated by Morten Kjær. This policy explains how we handle your data.

What we collect

We collect your email address and the meal and ingredient data you enter in the app. This data is stored securely in our database and used only to provide the app's functionality.

Family sharing

If you create or join a family, your meal plan data is shared with other members of that family group.

AI features

When you use AI ingredient suggestions, the meal name you enter is sent to OpenAI to generate suggestions. No personal information is sent.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are handled by RevenueCat and Apple. We do not store your payment information.

Third party services

We use Supabase for data storage, RevenueCat for subscriptions, and OpenAI for AI features.

Data deletion

To delete your account and all associated data, email us at dinrr.app@gmail.com.
